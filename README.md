<h1 align="center">unpack</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>We unpack the reading.</strong><br/>A reading check that's hard to fake.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://unpackreading.com">unpackreading.com</a></p>

---

Assigning a reading is easy. Knowing whether anyone actually read it is not. Quizzes get skimmed, summaries get copied, and "did you do the reading?" gets a nod.

unpack fixes that. Students read the text, then answer a few questions written for that exact passage. They only move on once they've genuinely tried.

## How it works

1. **Pick a reading.** Make a class, then paste a link or the text itself. Students join with your class code.
2. **Students join in seconds.** They type that code and their name. No accounts and no passwords.
3. **Questions that match the text.** AI writes a few open-ended questions tied to specific details in the passage, plus one that asks for the student's own take.
4. **Effort, not perfection.** Answers are checked for real effort, not for polish. A struggling reader who tries passes. Answers that dodge the reading don't.
5. **A nudge, not a red X.** If an answer falls short, the student gets a specific hint and tries again, up to three times.
6. **You stay in charge.** Students who are still stuck can send you a note. You can accept it, deny it, or give one more try, and you can see every answer they wrote.

## Why teachers like it

- **Hard to skip.** Every question is about this text, so blank, off-topic or copied answers won't get through.
- **Kind to struggling readers.** It grades effort, so students aren't punished for being early readers.
- **Light on your time.** You see who's done and who's stuck, and you only step in when a student asks.
- **Works with anything you read.** Articles, stories, or a passage you paste in. You aren't limited to a fixed library.
- **Built for kids.** Students never make an account, and teachers can delete a student and all of their work at any time. See [student data and privacy](docs/privacy.md).

## Reading on your own?

If you're 13 or older, you can sign in and use unpack for yourself. Paste anything you're reading, pick a level and how many questions you want, and check that you really got it. It's free for 5 new readings every 7 days.

## Pricing

Students are always free. Teachers can start free with one class, and a paid plan with more classes is on the way.
